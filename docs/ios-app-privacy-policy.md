# Smart Drink Fridge iOS App — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 8, 2026_

## Overview

The Smart Drink Fridge iOS app is a companion for a Smart Drink Fridge server operated and configured by the user. The app does not require a developer-operated cloud account.

## Data collected by the developer

The developer does not collect, store, sell, rent or use personal data, analytics, advertising identifiers, inventory data, scan data or account credentials from the app.

## Connection to your server

The app connects directly to the Smart Drink Fridge server URL entered by the user. Login credentials, inventory information, bookings, statistics and shopping-list data are exchanged only between the user's device and that user-configured server.

The operator of that server is responsible for its configuration, security, data processing and retention.

## Data stored on the device

The app may store the configured server address, authentication information and cached fridge data on the device so that the app can connect and show recent information. Sensitive authentication information is stored using Apple's secure platform facilities where applicable.

## Camera

Camera access is optional and requested only for barcode scanning. Camera images are processed on the device to recognize a barcode. The app does not upload or retain camera images.

## Widgets, Live Activities and Apple Watch

Widgets, Live Activities and the Apple Watch companion may display selected inventory or booking information supplied by the app. This data remains within the user's Apple devices and configured Smart Drink Fridge server.

## Demo mode

The built-in demo uses synthetic sample data stored locally and does not connect to an external server.

## Third-party advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising SDK and performs no cross-app or cross-website tracking.

## Data deletion

Users can remove locally stored app data by signing out, changing or removing the server configuration, or deleting the app. Server-side data must be managed on the user's own Smart Drink Fridge server.

## Children

The app is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality changes. The current version will remain available on this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions or correction requests, open an issue or discussion in the [Smart Drink Fridge project](https://github.com/DerRobin99/smart-drink-fridge).
